Free plan

Your 30-day plan to get more customers.

Do these in order. Each step is small. By day 30 you'll have ads, a page and follow-up emails working together.

Week 1 · Decide who and what

1. Pick one service to push. Choose the one that makes you the most money per job and that you enjoy doing.

2. Write your one-sentence offer. "We help [who] get [result] without [pain]." Example: "We fix leaking roofs in Denver with a fixed price before we start."

3. Pick one action. Book a call, request a quote, or claim a free trial. Just one. Every ad, page and email points to it.

Week 2 · Build the landing page

4. One page, no menu. A clear headline (the result), two lines of detail, your one action button and a few proof points: reviews, guarantee, years in business.

5. Make it work on a phone. Most of your visitors will be on one. Test it with your own thumb.

6. Ask for the least you need. An email or phone number is enough. Every extra box loses people.

Week 3 · Launch your first ad

7. Start small. A modest daily budget, shown only to people near you. You're learning, not winning yet.

8. One picture, one sentence, one button. The headline should say the result. The picture should show it.

9. Send the click to your page, not your homepage.

Week 4 · Follow up by email

10. Day 0: thank them and say what happens next.

11. Day 1–3: answer the top two doubts people have.

12. Day 5–7: show proof, then one friendly last call.

13. After the job: ask for a review.

Every week · Check three numbers

Cost per lead, how many leads become bookings, and how many bookings become paying jobs. Fix the weakest one first.
